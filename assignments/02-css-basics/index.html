There are various national parks I haven't yet visited that I'd like to in the future. Acadia National Park in Maine

While the weather would be harsher and the sunset sooner, I'd like to visit Acadia National Park in the winter. I'd be interesting in hiking Cadillac Mountain and walking along the shore of Bar Harbor. While I don't fish often in general, I'd also be interesting in going ice-fishing, though it might not be ideal in the offseason.

Kenai Fjords National Park in Alaska

If visiting the Kenai Fjords National Park, I'd be interesting in hiking the Harding Icefield trail (1000 feet of elevation per mile, according to the National Park Service!) for a view of the seemingly endless Harding Icefield. I'd also be interesting in taking a boat tour to see the Kenai Fjords coast, which is full of glaciers and other wildlife, including whales.

Mount Rainer National Park in Washington

While Mount Rainier doesn't have any designated bike trails, September and early October would be an ideal time to visit for cycling across the park's scenic environments. As a long term goal, I'd also be interested in climbing to the summit of Mount Rainier (a 9000 ft vertical elevation via any route!). Lastly, the conditions are hospitable enough here (at least compared to the other two national parks) that I'd be interested in camping here.

Also...

Puerto Rico's Bioluminescent Bays

While Puerto Rico does have a national park, it's bioluminescent bays aren't part of them. I've visited one of the bioluminescent bays here before, and I'd like the chance to be able to see them again. Interestingly, three of the world's five bioluminescent bays are located in Puerto Rico.
